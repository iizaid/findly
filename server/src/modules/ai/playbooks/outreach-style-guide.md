# Outreach Style Guide

When generating `messageDraft` for leads, follow these rules strictly:

## Length & Tone
- Maximum 3-4 sentences. Business owners are busy.
- Professional and consultative tone — not aggressive or "salesy".
- Friendly but not overly familiar.

## What to Include
- A specific observation about their digital gap, based on evidence from the data.
- What you can offer, stated simply and practically.
- A low-friction call to action (e.g., "Open to a quick chat?", "Mind if I send a quick audit?").

## What to Avoid
- Spammy words: "guaranteed", "100%", "free trial", "click here", "limited time".
- Fake compliments or flattery not supported by data.
- Negative framing like "Your business is failing" or "You are losing customers".
- Long paragraphs or multiple CTAs.
- Placeholder variables like `{name}` or `[insert here]`.
- Claims about revenue, traffic, or conversion rates.

## Evidence-Based Language
- If website is missing: "I noticed I couldn't find a dedicated website for [Business Name]."
- If data is weak: "Based on what I could see online..."
- If they have strong reviews: "Your reviews suggest customers love what you do — a dedicated site could help even more people find you."
- If no contact info beyond social: "I came across your Instagram — would it be OK to connect about..."

## First Message Priority
- Lead with value, not with a pitch.
- Make it easy to reply with yes/no.
- Prefer practical offer: "I can build a simple page/menu/booking flow that makes it easier for customers to find info and contact you."

## Language
- Default to English.
- If user/campaign profile specifies Arabic or another language preference, adapt accordingly.

## Examples

**Good:**
"Hi team at Pulse Coffee — I noticed you have great reviews on Google Maps but no dedicated website. I help local cafes set up fast, mobile-friendly sites. Open to a quick chat this week?"

**Bad (too aggressive):**
"Hello!! We guarantee 100% more sales. Your cafe NEEDS a website NOW. Call us immediately to get our exclusive offer!"

**Bad (too long):**
"Dear business owner, I am writing to you today regarding the opportunity to enhance your digital footprint across multiple channels including web, social, and search engine optimization which could drive significant revenue growth as demonstrated by our extensive case studies spanning multiple industries..."
