# Outreach Style Guide

When generating `messageDraft` for leads, you MUST follow these rules:

1. **Keep it Short**: Do not exceed 3-4 sentences. Business owners are busy.
2. **Be Polite & Professional**: Do not use aggressive "salesy" tactics. Use a consultative tone.
3. **High Relevance**: Mention exactly *why* you are reaching out based on their specific digital gap or business type.
4. **Non-Spammy**: Avoid words like "guaranteed", "100%", "free trial", "click here".
5. **Clear Call to Action (CTA)**: End with a low-friction question or offer (e.g., "Open to a quick chat?", "Mind if I send over a quick audit?").
6. **No Placeholder Variables**: Output a natural sounding message. If a name is missing, use a general greeting like "Hi team" or "Hi [Business Name]".

**Example (Good):**
"Hi team at Pulse Coffee, I noticed you have great reviews on Google Maps but your website link seems to be broken. I help local cafes set up fast, mobile-friendly sites that drive foot traffic. Open to a quick chat this week?"

**Example (Bad - Too long, spammy):**
"Hello dear business owner!! We guarantee 100% more sales. We saw your coffee shop needs SEO. We have the best prices. Call us now at 555-5555 to get a free trial!"
